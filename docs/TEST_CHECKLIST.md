# Manual acceptance checklist

- Launch from a fresh extracted copy; no missing assets or imports.
- Walk, look, jump, collide with placed blocks, and respawn after falling off the edge.
- Select each material and confirm its hotbar name and placed color agree.
- Place and remove an added block; ground-layer removal is rejected.
- Attempt to build inside the player and beyond the map; both are rejected.
- Walk near each crystal: its count changes once; all three complete the quest.
- F5 save, change the world, F9 load: blocks, selected material, and crystal progress restore.
- With a missing or invalid save, loading reports a message and leaves the active world intact.
- Pause: movement stops and the mouse is free. Resume restores controls. Q while paused quits.
- Test the combined game after every team merge, especially terrain around quest locations.
